Cookie Policy

Last updated · 22.08.2026

1. The short version

This site sets no cookies. It stores one item in your browser to remember whether you prefer English or Spanish, and it loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

2. What we store in your browser

NameTypePurposeDuration
lang Local storage (not a cookie) Remembers your language choice so the site opens in the right one next time. Until you clear your browser data

This is a strictly necessary functional preference that you set yourself, so it does not require consent under Article 22.2 of the LSSI-CE. It contains nothing but the text en or es, and it is never sent to us or to anyone else.

3. Third-party services

Google Fonts

The typefaces on this site are served by Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests those font files from Google's servers, which means your IP address is transmitted to Google. Google states that it does not set cookies or use this data for advertising. See Google's privacy policy.

If you would prefer no data to reach Google at all, the fonts can be hosted directly on this site instead. Contact us and we will tell you the current arrangement.

Hosting

Our hosting provider processes standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and to keep the site running. These are not used to identify or profile visitors.

4. What we do not use

  • No Google Analytics or any other analytics package.
  • No advertising or remarketing pixels.
  • No social media trackers or embedded feeds.
  • No chat widgets or session recording.

If that changes, this page will be updated and a consent banner will be shown before any non-essential technology is loaded.

5. Managing storage in your browser

You can clear or block local storage and cookies in your browser settings. Doing so will simply mean the site forgets your language preference. Instructions: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge.

6. More information

See our Privacy Policy for how we handle personal data, or write to david@365berries.com.
