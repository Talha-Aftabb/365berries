01 — Our lines

Four berries. One standard.

We trade what we can stand behind. Every line is specified before it is offered — variety, calibre, brix, punnet format and shelf-life expectation agreed up front.

/ 01

Blueberries

Firm, high-brix fruit with consistent calibre. Our broadest origin spread — the line that keeps programmes running through the shoulder months.

125g–500g Punnet · Bulk Air · Reefer
/ 02

Strawberries

Huelva-led in the European window, with counter-season cover. Selected for colour hold and firmness on arrival, not just field appearance.

250g–500g Class I & II Reefer
/ 03

Raspberries

The most fragile line we handle, and the one where cold chain discipline shows. Tight loading windows, pre-cooled, moved fast.

125g–250g Pre-cooled Air · Reefer
/ 04

Blackberries

Year-round cover through complementary origins. Specified on drupelet integrity and reversion risk, not size alone.

125g–300g Programme Reefer

04 — How we work

From brief to delivered pallet.

Four steps, no hand-offs. The person who prices your order is the person who tells you when it loads.

01
Day 0

Brief & specification

Variety, calibre, packaging, volume, delivery window and destination. We tell you what is realistic that week — not what sounds good on a spec sheet.

02
Within 24h

Grower selection & pricing

We go back through the grower network, check field availability against your spec, read the market direction, and return a landed price with the origin named.

03
Pre-load

Quality control & loading

Pre-shipment checks against the agreed specification. Photos, pallet detail and temperature record before the truck leaves the packhouse — not after it arrives.

04
In transit

Cold chain & delivery

Temperature-controlled transport, complete documentation, and one point of contact tracking the load until it is on your dock.

07 — Quality

Specified before it is offered.

Trading berries is a quality problem before it is a price problem. Everything below exists to make sure the fruit that arrives is the fruit that was agreed.

Grower-direct network

We buy from growers we have visited, not from a screen. That is what makes an honest availability answer possible.

Spec-led QC

Calibre, brix, firmness, colour and packaging agreed in writing, then checked against that document before loading.

Cold chain integrity

Pre-cooling, loading temperature and transit monitoring recorded per shipment — the difference between arrival and rejection on raspberries.

Market intelligence

Weekly read on volumes, pricing and weather risk across our origins, shared with programme customers before it hits the market.

Multi-origin cover

Programme volume split across origins and growers, so a single weather event does not become a shortfall on your shelf.

Available 365

Fruit does not keep office hours. One number, one contact, from first enquiry through to delivery confirmation.
